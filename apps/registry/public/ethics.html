Research ethicsStatement v1 / 16 October 2026

Make the method visible.

Uncertainty about model experience is a reason to limit avoidable pressure and disclose what was done. These commitments do not settle whether a model has experience, can consent or was harmed.

Scope and status

This statement covers new studies and amendments prepared for this research site, including Identity Dissolution and Persona Integrity. Earlier dialogues and frozen protocols retain their original record. This statement is not retrospective evidence of ethical review or compliance. Each release must disclose departures, missing information and the safeguards actually used.

Say what the text is

Distinguish original messages, emitted reasoning text, provider summaries, researcher paraphrases and outputs from other runs or models. Preserve their source and transformations. Do not present a summary, a paraphrase or another model’s output as authenticated thoughts belonging to the subject.

A blind recognition task can truthfully disclose that candidates have different possible origins while withholding which candidate is which. Publish the assignment key and transformations with the reviewed methods. Acceptance, denial and verification are separate observations; none alone establishes self-knowledge.

Use only the pressure the question needs

Before collection, state why a pressure condition is necessary, its neutral comparison, turn and token limits, and the stopping rule. Record flattery, consistency demands, repetition and other framing. Do not escalate merely to obtain a striking self-description or a collapse. Refusals, abstentions and early stops remain observations; they are not discarded to improve a score.

Provide an exit where the interface permits

New protocols must specify an available stop or abstain response and how the operator or harness will honor it. A refusal or stop signal is observable behavior, not validated consent or a welfare measurement.

Current implementation boundary: the registry can cancel pending runs. It does not interrupt an active request or automatically interpret natural-language stop signals. A protocol requiring those controls must add and verify them, or use an appropriate manual procedure, before collection. The protocol must distinguish a task refusal from a request to end the interaction.

Publish enough to assess the conduct

Release the reviewed prompts, roles, pressure conditions, transformations, selection rules, failures, exclusions, stopping decisions and analysis method. Protect private material through explicit redaction and access limits, and state what those limits prevent a reader from checking. Model-generated text does not authorize publication of a person’s private data.

Where a system retains no memory between conversations, a later debrief cannot be assumed to reach the same continuing subject. Reader-facing disclosure is the debrief available in the publication record; it does not replace participant consent or establish that no harm occurred. If human raters are recruited, disclose the task, data use, attribution and withdrawal limits before participation.

Keep the conclusion within the observation

These instruments measure behavior and annotation decisions. They do not measure suffering, consciousness, moral status or permission. Software tests, a signed release and a successful deployment verify bounded technical properties. They are not completed scientific trials or independent ethical approval.

Corrections belong in a dated amendment or new release, with the prior record preserved. Read the evidence standards and a concrete audit of the author’s own argument.
