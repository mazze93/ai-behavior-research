Let the argument lose.

An argument about integrity needs a method that can expose its own dependencies. The author’s claim graph does that, and still leaves work unresolved.

What is counted

The engine folds recorded events into a claim’s current standing. It counts distinct, active, declared supporting frames. The author’s writing in the provenance-only frame contributes zero; an attached check contributes only after a recorded outcome says it held. Contradictions remain visible, and refutation is an explicit judgment rather than arithmetic subtraction.

A frame label is a claim about independence, not proof of it. The engine calls two or more supporting frames “robust”; a one-frame claim is “fragile.” Inspecting the result after removing a frame is still necessary. Two frames do not imply that the claim remains robust after either is removed.
