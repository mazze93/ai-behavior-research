Rigor belongs in the infrastructure.

Scientific rigor should be a property of the tools, not a burden each researcher rebuilds alone. This page says how far these tools have got.

This is a status page, generated from checked records. Most of what an open research lab needs is not built here yet. Today the public can read, verify and reproduce; only the owner can design, run, assign and release. Nothing below is a research finding.

The research object

Each investigation is one versioned record that runs through nine stages:

Question → Hypothesis → Protocol → Execution → Observation → Analysis → Claim → Review → Publication

A stage is absent, draft, recorded, frozen or not applicable. A stage can be recorded only when the stages it rests on are settled. A claim must link its evidence and state its limits. An evidence grade cannot exceed what the recorded stages support. Publication is recorded only as a named person’s decision, and peer review or a DOI only as an outside event with its own record.

A record that passes these checks is consistent. That does not make it correct, and a hash identifies bytes, not truth. Read the contract or download the three records below.
