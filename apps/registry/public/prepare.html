What these estimates mean
Analysis is post-hoc and exploratory, even if a source protocol says confirmatory. Intervals resample requested-seed means; seed labels do not establish independent draws. At least three contributing seeds are required. Authored variants remain fixed, and selective missingness and output length can change the result.
Smoke tests receive no intervals and support no research findings. Consciousness, persistent identity and causal generalization are not measured.
No manuscript, dataset or DOI is automatically published. Review authorship, sources, reuse terms, methods and limitations before any separate deposit.
