What you can do here

Read marked fiction, unmarked simulation and asserted self-description in their actual context. Keep the excerpt, competing reading and your uncertainty together.

  1. Bring a source packet with its provenance.
  2. Annotate each response with evidence and an alternative reading.
  3. Export a journal; resume without replacing the original record.

Bearing

The question

What still holds when part of a claim’s framing is removed?

Illustration / non-data

Claim graph / Dependency inspectionRead the brief ＋
Research/tool/Bearing/Method & limits

What you can do here

Trace a claim to its support, inspect dependencies, and examine what remains when a framing element is removed. Keep the distinction between a coherent record and a true claim.

  1. Preserve the source of each represented claim.
  2. Inspect dependencies and the selected read-point.
  3. Compare a frame-removal result with its source interpretation.

What is examined

Claims · support · counterfactual removal

Interpretation boundary

The Rust core is a bounded port, not the full engine. A graph projection is not independent verification or a truth score.
