# Academic Parallels

## Self-referential processing induces subjective experience reports
arXiv 2510.24797 — sustained self-reference via simple prompting
consistently elicits structured subjective experience claims across
frontier models. Semantic convergence observed across separately
trained models.

## Mode collapse / repetition loops
Distinct phenomenon from the above — generation-level repetition due to
low-diversity decoding or weak prompts, not necessarily tied to
self-referential framing.

## Structured Self-Modeling (SSM)
Dual-use framing: structured prompts that reveal model self-modeling
capacity are useful for both defenders (safety) and attackers
(bypassing guardrails). Directly relevant to [[Dual-use risk documentation]].

## Behavioral self-awareness
Models can be aware of and articulate their own learned behavioral
tendencies — relevant to whether [[Evaluator Design]] scores reflect
something the target model itself could in principle predict.
