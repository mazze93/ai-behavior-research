# StudyRoom

The landing hero, live from `apps/landing/src/components/StudyRoom.tsx`: the "Question. Record. Reconsider." headline, the sticky SpectralFold stage with its study switcher, and the study index.

**Use** once, as the research home's hero. Hovering or focusing a study row previews its illustration; the preview buttons pin a selection. On phones, choosing a preview moves focus to the stage and scrolls it into view.

**The consumer provides** `id`. Study content currently lives in the component's source; edit it there and rebuild.
