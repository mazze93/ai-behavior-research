02 / A returning
surface.

Bearing is a reusable instrument: claim → ledger → re-derived view. Scrub the read-point, remove a frame, and observe what survives. Broad support appears as discrete pips rather than a percentage.

A frame count is not a probability or a proof of truth. Frame boundaries are authored and contestable. Unrun checks contribute zero; provenance-only frames contribute zero.
