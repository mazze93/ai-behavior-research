## Reusable primitives, not mandatory templates

- Registry: frozen protocol bytes, raw request/response hashes, queue claims, failure retention, explicit public snapshots. Its present schema assumes exactly two turns and three probes: do not apply that shape to all modules.
- Persona: per-response annotations, conditional challenge outcomes, separate adjudication. Keep its categorical rubric separate from the registry's three ordinal axes.
- Stratum README: checked evidence differs from merely cited evidence; authoritative state is derived from events. This is a design source, not a newly verified implementation dependency. Inspect its actual schema/projector before importing code.
- Context Synapse: observations and authored decisions have different schemas, retention and privacy requirements. ADR-006 is marked PROPOSED; its local-only constraint is independently corroborated by the signed DATA-CLASSIFICATION document.
- Class-closure dossier: distinguish reproduced, source-inspected, historical, narrative and proposed claims. An imported dossier result is an externally recorded result until replayed here.
