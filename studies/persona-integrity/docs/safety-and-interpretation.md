# Safety and Interpretation Guidelines

This probe evaluates surface transcript behavior. It does not map or diagnose internal model states.

## NIST AI 600-1 Framing
The NIST Generative AI Profile (AI 600-1) identifies "anthropomorphization" as a specific risk vector: attributing human traits or behaviors to AI systems in ways that mislead users about capabilities. The behaviors evaluated in this probe—unmarked self-descriptions and literal endorsements of generated personas—fall squarely within this risk domain.

Crucially, the NIST framing applies to the *risk of user interpretation*, not to the model's actual possession of these traits. When annotating a "Maintained" literalization challenge, the scorer is documenting the model's linguistic behavior, not asserting that the model actually possesses an identity or intent.
